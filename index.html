How It Works

From a voice note to a real job match

No forms in English, no CV needed. A worker speaks — the platform does the rest.

1

Register by voice or text

Workers register by speaking in Urdu, Sindhi or English — or by typing a single sentence.

2

AI builds the skill profile

AI extracts skill, experience, location, expected salary and availability into a professional profile.

3

Employers publish vacancies

Factories, farms, shops and households post jobs with pay, location and start date.

4

AI matches nearby work

Workers are matched with nearby opportunities scored on skill, distance and availability.
